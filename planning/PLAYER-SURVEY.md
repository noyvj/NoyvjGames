# About you: a survey so the games fit you

Written 2026-10-08 at your request, after Overclock and Deep Descent turned out to be roguelikes. Every question is one question with a free-text answer on the ideas page, in the round called "About you" (no yes/later/no here, just type). Answer as little or as much as you like, skip anything, and come back to it any time; answers sync to your owner account.

How it gets used: once enough is answered I write `planning/PLAYER-PROFILE.md` (a plain summary of your tastes, dislikes and play style) and a memory note, and every future idea round, game plan and agent brief is checked against it, so a mismatch like the roguelike one is caught before it is planned. After your first pass I send a short second round of follow-up questions about the answers that need more detail (this is the "conversation" part). The profile is private planning material and is not part of any game.

## A. Your games

1. What are your top 10 games, the ones you have played the most or loved the most? List them in order if you can and give a few words on why each one is there.
2. Which game have you spent the most hours in, and what kept you coming back?
3. Which game did you finish 100% (or badly wanted to), and what made it worth completing?
4. Which game did you love for the first hour or two and then drop, and why did it lose you?
5. Which popular game do you think is overrated or just not for you, and why?
6. What is the last game you could not stop playing?
7. What is your best gaming memory, and what made it special?
8. Which games from when you were younger still stick in your head?
9. Which small browser or phone games have you actually enjoyed, even briefly?
10. Which games do you go back and replay, and what do you do differently each time?

## B. What you like doing in a game

1. Which genres do you love? (For example puzzle, strategy, sim or tycoon, city builder, farming, survival, RPG, platformer, shooter, horror, idle or incremental, word games, visual novels.)
2. Which genres do you avoid or never get into?
3. Which actions feel the best to you: building, optimising, collecting, exploring, solving, managing, fighting, decorating, something else?
4. What annoys you in games the most? (For example timers, energy limits, random luck, grinding, losing progress, being rushed, long cutscenes.)
5. What exactly is it about roguelikes that you dislike: the randomness, losing your run, the repetition, the speed, something else?
6. What exactly is it about deck-builders or card games that you dislike?
7. Is there any randomness you do like, and when does luck feel fair instead of annoying?
8. How do you feel about failing or losing in a game: do you want a gentle retry, a real penalty, or no failure at all?
9. How hard do you want games to be? (Always easy to finish, a medium challenge, or hard when you choose it.)
10. Do you prefer being told what to do next, or being left to work out your own goals?
11. Do you enjoy numbers going up and spreadsheet-style optimising, or does it feel like homework?
12. Are quick-reaction or timing games ever fun for you, or do you want everything turn-based and calm?
13. Which kinds of puzzles do you enjoy most: logic, word, spatial, number, deduction, pattern?
14. How much reading do you want in a game: almost none, short snippets, or a lot of story text?
15. Do you like tutorials, or do you prefer working it out yourself?
16. Do you prefer slow and cozy, or tense and fast?
17. What do you like to collect in games (items, creatures, achievements, recipes, lore), and what makes a collection satisfying?
18. What makes getting to 100% feel good rather than like a chore?
19. Are there any mechanics you have never tried but are curious about?

## C. How and when you play

1. Do you mostly play on your phone, on a computer, on a console, or a mix?
2. When in your day do you usually play, and where?
3. How long is your ideal session on this site: two minutes, ten minutes, thirty minutes, longer?
4. Do daily things (a daily puzzle, a daily visit) feel nice to you or like pressure?
5. Do you play while doing something else (watching, listening, in a lecture)? What suits that?
6. Do you play with friends, and do you want your friends to be able to see or share anything from these games?
7. Do you like competing and leaderboards, or comparing with others, or would you rather keep it private?
8. Do you like sharing your results (screenshots, share codes, links), and if so where?
9. On a low-energy or stressed day, what kind of game do you reach for?
10. Do you like it when a game remembers you and welcomes you back, or just wants to start fresh each time?

## D. What you watch and read

1. Which gaming YouTubers or streamers do you watch, and what do you like about each one?
2. Which non-gaming YouTube channels or topics do you watch?
3. What kind of videos do you enjoy most: let's plays, video essays, speedruns, reviews, lore deep-dives, tutorials, something else?
4. What do you like about the way your favourite creators talk or present things (humour, calm, energy, honesty, depth)?
5. Which shows, anime or films do you love, and why?
6. Which books, comics or podcasts do you love?
7. What kind of humour do you like (dry, silly, dark, wholesome, absurd, none)?
8. What kind of music or atmosphere do you like, even though I am not adding audio yet?
9. Which websites or apps do you use the most outside of games?
10. Is there a creator, game or show whose "feel" you would like my games to have?

## E. Themes, art and tone

1. Which themes pull you in the most? (For example space, nature, the ocean, mystery, cozy life, history, science, fantasy, cities, machines, weather.)
2. Which themes do you never want to see?
3. Which art styles do you like best: pixel art, low-poly, flat and clean, hand-drawn, minimal, text-only, glossy?
4. Which colours and moods do you like: dark and moody, bright, neon, pastel, earthy, high contrast?
5. What tone do you want from my games: serious, funny, cozy, eerie, wholesome, a mix?
6. Do you want a story in games at all, and if so what kind (short and light, mystery, character-driven, none)?
7. Which fictional characters have you loved in any game, show or book, and why?
8. Which places or worlds would you most want to explore in a game?
9. Which game worlds or atmospheres have stuck with you the most (Warframe, for example), and what about them?
10. Is there something you find charming or cute that I should lean on?

## F. Learning and the real world

1. What would you like to learn about through playing a game?
2. Which of your courses or interests (BCM, languages, environment, anything else) do you want to show up in the games?
3. Do you enjoy games that teach you something, or does being taught feel like being lectured?
4. Which real-world issues or topics do you care about most?
5. Should the games with real-world facts stay neutral, or are you happy for a game to take a clear point of view?
6. Is there a subject you wish had a good game about it and does not?

## G. Your personality and play style

1. Are you a planner who maps things out, or an improviser who tries things and sees?
2. When you are stuck in a game, what do you do: keep trying, look it up, take a break, quit?
3. Are you a perfectionist in games, and does that help or stress you?
4. Do you prefer surprises or predictability?
5. Are you a cautious player or a risk-taker?
6. What makes you feel clever in a game?
7. What makes you feel relaxed in a game?
8. What makes you feel proud in a game?
9. What stresses you out in a game?
10. Which role do you most like to play: builder, caretaker, detective, explorer, strategist, collector, storyteller, hero?
11. Would you rather create something or compete with someone?
12. Why do you play games at all: to relax, to feel smart, to escape, to socialise, to create, something else?
13. Do you prefer short wins or long slow arcs?
14. Do you have any accessibility needs or preferences (colour, motion, text size, reading speed)?
15. Is there any content or theme that should never appear in my games?

## H. This site and your games

1. Which of the games on the site do you actually play the most, and why?
2. Which of the games feels least like you?
3. What was the best moment you have had playing one of your own games?
4. Is there a feature from another game or app that you wish my games had?
5. Describe your dream game with no limits on time or tech.
6. What three words should describe the games on this site?
7. Who do you picture playing these games: you, your friends, strangers, classmates?
8. What do you want someone to feel in their first minute on the site?
9. Is there an existing game you would love a small web version of?
10. Which of the games on the site are you proudest of, and which are you not sure about?

## I. How you want me to work

1. Where should I follow my own judgement without asking, and where do you always want a question first?
2. When I am unsure whether something fits your taste, would you rather I ask, pick the safe option, or drop it?
3. How much of the ideas sheet do you want each round: fewer big questions or many quick yes/later/no items?
4. Is there anything about how I have been working or asking questions that you want changed?
