# Dream game: design notes from your answers (working name: "dream game")

Status: groundwork only, no code. Written 2026-10-09 from your answers to FOR-YOU Fg1 to Fg25 and your own description (survey H5). Name: "dream game" for now, ideate a name later (Fg1). No timeline (Fg25). It is an open-ended, ever-growing game with no planned ending (Fg21), so every note below is a starting point.

## What it is
A cozy dark-forest settlement game that starts as one cabin in the woods and grows into a large town of buildings, each run by a person (a "keeper") with their own quests. Everything you do feeds the settlement; it is the home base and the main loop (Fg2).

## Look and feel (Fg4, Fg5, Fg19)
- **Side view, like a cross-section of each building** so you can see inside it for that building's own activity. The settlement grows outward from a simple cabin. Not top-down, not 3D.
- Dark, almost black or very dark green trees, constant night, with the seasons still changing (leaves fall, orange tint, snow). Low-poly dark and foggy style.
- **Fog** is mostly atmosphere: it hides unexplored areas and clears when explored, but is always present in the background.
- What lives in the fog: machines and strange remnants (Fg6, recommendation taken).

## People (Fg7, Fg10, Fg11, Fg16, Fg17)
- Start with humans; robots are researched later in a building, through a whole production line before you reach them (Fg7).
- About 6 keepers at launch, each with a real questline, more added over time (Fg11).
- Every keeper has favourites and hated things that they hint at in dialogue (Fg10); gifts are found and crafted objects so collecting feeds friendship.
- One main story plus a set of quests for every keeper tied to what their building needs (Fg17), not a pile of unrelated branching lines.
- Very interconnected between players if they want: they can help each other, fight together, and in the lore all the places exist in the same forest (Fg16). Needs the shared run codes and friend ties first; always optional, no chat.

## First five minutes (Fg20)
You wake in the cabin to someone knocking: the first keeper, a builder who can raise new buildings. His first quest is for wood; then he builds his own workshop as the first building.

## Danger and failure (Fg8, Fg9)
Cozy by default, no constant threat, no forced combat. You can seek out fights for faster resources, and doing so can provoke a retaliation with consequences. Nothing ends the game; consequences are fixable.

## Buildings and puzzles (Fg3, Fg12, Fg13)
- Each building has its own puzzle kind whose levels give permanent upgrades. You asked for a bunch of puzzle ideas per building to choose from: see the new "Dream game" round on the ideas page (file `planning/DREAM-GAME-IDEAS.md`).
- First piece to build as its own small game: the per-building puzzles (Fg3), since they can stand alone.
- Mining mode is a separate screen opened from a mine building, with the big skill tree and bunker sections that become building specialisations (Fg13).

## Playing in short or long sessions (Fg14, Fg15)
Every building offers a 5 to 15 minute task with deeper optimisation always available. The city keeps producing while you are away, with a generous cap and no pressure to return.

## Other site games (Fg22, Fg18)
Not forced inside it. Some pair naturally (Canopy for wood) but the link is only that a game's achievement percentage can give a small buff. No site game is closest in feel (Fg18).

## Scope and platform (Fg21, Fg24, Fg25)
