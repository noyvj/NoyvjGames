Settings

One place for the preferences that are spread across the hub and the games. Almost everything here is stored on this device; the account section at the bottom talks to the server.

Look and feel

Applies to the hub and every game. If you are signed in, the theme follows your account to your other devices.

Switches off the hub's drifting background and card animations, and turns on each game's own "reduce motion" option. A game's own Settings panel can still change it afterwards.

Lite mode switches off animations, blurs, moving backgrounds and big shadows on the hub and in every game.

It turns itself on, with a note, when this browser reports 2 GB of memory or less, two processor cores or fewer, or a data-saver setting. If you are signed in, your choice follows your account. It sits next to each game's own "reduce motion" switch and does not change it.

Warm night colours and half-asleep mode are saved on this device and apply to the hub now; each game picks them up once it loads the same files.

Defaults for every game

Sets each game's text size to the same value. Le Champ de Mots has no text-size setting yet, so it is left out.

When on, a game uploads your progress to the save server every five minutes while it is open (to your account if you are signed in, otherwise under a save code). It is off unless you turn it on. Each game's Save panel can still switch it per game.

Translates the buttons and messages every game shares: the save panel, confirmation boxes, tutorial cards and achievement labels. Each game's own story and menus stay in English. English is the default. The Spanish and French texts were written with machine help and have not been proof-read by a person yet. This choice is kept on this device only.

Privacy

Off by default. When on, the hub adds one to a single running visit total each time you open it: no IP address, page or device detail is sent. The same switch is in the hub's footer. See Terms & Privacy.

When on, the hub skips its community statistics (the highlights line and the leader captions under the sort menu). Ratings, saves and anything you ask for still load.

Games stored for offline use

Download a game from its card on the hub (Show details, then Download for offline) and it keeps working without a connection, including its Python runtime. Each game's files and the shared runtime are listed with their size here, and you can remove any of them. Pages the app keeps anyway are never removed, and the shared runtime stays while another downloaded game needs it. A site update can clear downloads: the card then says it needs downloading again.

Checking what is stored…

Your data on this device

Download everything stored here as a file

The file lists every setting and local save code this site keeps in this browser. It leaves out your sign-in token. Save codes can open and overwrite that save, so keep the file private.

Delete the offline copy of the site

Only removes the stored copies of pages and the Python runtime (this includes games you downloaded for offline use); your settings and saves stay. Games will need a connection again until you have reopened them.

Your account on the server

You are not signed in on this device. Sign in from the hub to download or delete what the server holds for your account.

Download everything the server holds for your account as a file

Your saves (with their codes), achievements, feedback you wrote while signed in, leaderboard rows, board privacy choice, What's New votes, email and synced settings. It leaves out your password and sign-in tokens. Save codes can open and overwrite their saves, so keep the file private. Star ratings and answer reports are not linked to any account, so they are not in it.

Delete my account

This permanently deletes your account and, from the server, every save and slot under it, your leaderboard rows and board settings, feedback you wrote while signed in and your What's New votes. It cannot be undone and there is no way to get any of it back. Download your data first if you want a copy. Saves stored only on this device, and ratings or answer reports (which were never linked to you), stay.

Clear local data

Reset the hub's own preferences (filters, tour, notices, shortcuts, motion). Keeps your sign-in and every game's data.
Erase everything this site stored on this device: sign-in, settings, local save codes and local game data.

Saves kept on the server under your account are not touched, but a save code you have not claimed to an account is gone for good once it is erased here, so export first if you might need one.
