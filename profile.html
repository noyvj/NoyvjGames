Who can see my profile

Not available yet: there is no friends list on NoyvjGames, so this option switches on when friends exist. Until then a profile is either private or public.

When public, anyone with your link can see exactly what is listed below the line “What others see”: your username, the month you joined, your favourite game, your badges, your achievement count for each game, your total time played and your longest streaks. Never your email, saves, save codes or password. Set it back to private at any time and the page disappears for everyone else straight away.

Counts the achievements in the saves already on your account and raises your profile to match, for games you played before profiles existed. It never lowers a number, and time played cannot be worked out from saves, so that only grows from now on.
