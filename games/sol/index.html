Ecological Health
          
i
Extraction pollutes the planet over time. Below 10% health, output drops 25%; at 0%, production stops entirely until it recovers. Recyclers slow the decline, and a trade route from a stable planet can ship in relief.

Terraforming
          
i
Terraforming fills on its own from sustained economic and ecological balance — it isn't a separate resource you spend, so keeping ecology healthy over time is what drives this bar up.

Research — Near Bodies Tier

        
i
Research unlocks new destinations in tiers, not one planet at a time — reaching the Near Bodies tier opens the Moon and Mars together, and later tiers open several more bodies at once.

Governor — Priority & Budget
          
i
The Governor controls how a planet manages itself while you're away — Priority decides what it optimizes for (Growth, Balance, or Ecology), and Budget sets what share of its resources it spends on that goal.

        

        

          
          
          
        

        
Budget: 50% of Iron
