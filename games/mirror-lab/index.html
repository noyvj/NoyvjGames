                  Sensors

                    How to read the bench
                    • ▬ A white beam is a thin solid line. An S (short) beam is short dashes marked with an S. An L (long) beam is long dashes marked with an L.
                    • A sensor is a hollow eight-sided lamp until a beam of the right kind reaches it, then it is filled and ticked. A letter S or L in its corner means only that kind lights it. A sensor never stops a beam.
                    • A dashed square is a slot where a piece may go. Solid grey blocks are walls. The square with an arrow is the emitter. A fixed mirror has bolts and cannot be moved.
                    • Tap a placed mirror or prism to flip it. A splitter lets the beam go on and sends a copy to the left of its travel.
                    • A sensor drawn with a cross and a dashed edge is a decoy: it must stay dark. If a beam reaches it, it fills in and shows an exclamation mark, and the sensor list says so in words.
                    • An S filter (a ringed circle with an S) turns a white beam short and stops a long one; an L filter does the opposite. An absorber (a dark square with hatching) stops any beam.
                    • A dotted frame on a slot is a hint ghost: the piece the hint suggests, drawn see-through. It is only ever a suggestion.
