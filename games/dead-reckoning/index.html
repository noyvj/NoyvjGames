Dead Reckoning

You know your speed, your heading and the time. You do not know where the sea has put you. Plot a course, sail it, then see how far the truth drifted from your estimate.

Charts

Pick a chart. A chapter opens once you have made landfall on four of the one before. Your stars stay with each chart.

Practice

A fresh chart every time, always solvable, never scored against the campaign. Every one you sail counts toward your practice total. The code under the chart's name replays the same sea.

Daily chart

One chart a day, the same sea for everyone, always solvable. It never touches your campaign stars or your practice total. There are no streaks: skip as many days as you like, and every past day stays open.

A dot means not played yet; stars show your best result for that date. Any of them can be played now.

Captain's log

The opening line of every chart you have tried, and how the ones you cleared ended. The Story button, bottom left, hides it.
