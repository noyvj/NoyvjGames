Manage a receiving region as displacement pressure rises. Whether that pressure becomes a crisis or a manageable transition comes down to how well the region prepared.

Institutional preparedness, not the scale of arrivals alone, is what determines outcomes. Uganda's refugee policy — granting land access and the right to work and move freely, integrated alongside host communities — is widely cited by policy researchers as a model of how institutional capacity can turn large-scale displacement into manageable, even mutually beneficial, coexistence.
