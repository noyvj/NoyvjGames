Teach the Machine

A worn station workshop, and Pip, a small sorting machine that barely speaks. You choose what Pip may look at, press Train, and see how it does on cases it has never seen. When it gets one wrong, you can open it up and see why. Nothing is timed and nothing is lost.

Lessons

Each shelf is eight lessons. Five cleared lessons on a shelf open the next one; inside a shelf, any order. A lesson with a double border is cleared; "best features" means you cleared it looking at exactly the right things.

Failure Notebook

Every wrong answer is one of five kinds of mistake. The first time you open a Why on an example of one, it is written here.

Pip's lessons

Nothing yet. Clear a lesson and a card of what Pip learned is kept here.
