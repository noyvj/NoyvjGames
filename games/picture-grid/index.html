Picture Grid

A closed gallery of small pictures on a quiet station. Each frame is blank until you work out what it shows from the numbers on its rows and columns. Nothing is timed and nothing can go wrong.

The Long Gallery

Every picture you solve is hung here, with its name. Open one to see it large. Each gallery opens when six pictures of the one before are hung; inside a gallery, pick any frame in any order.

Describe this picture in words

Left and right arrow keys step through the hung pictures, Escape closes this view.

Achievements

Every one can be earned in any order, on any difficulty, with no luck and no deadline, and each shows how close you are.
