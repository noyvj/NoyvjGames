Lexis

You are the communications officer. Planet 1 is sending something. Work out what it means, then answer.

Planet 1: the quiet beacon

Received transmissions

Each signal is a row of marks: a short tick or a long bar. Watch what the station does when one arrives.

    Notebook

    Write what you think each group of marks means. Guesses are free and nothing here is marked right or wrong. Tick some entries and press Check to learn how many of them are right, but not which.

      Transmit

      Build a signal from marks, or drop in a group you have already seen. The station answers in its own terms.

      Planet 2: the market world

      A tray sits on the counter. Signs here are made of parts. Watch what arrives on the tray when a sign is sent.

      Received transmissions

        Notebook

        Each part of a sign has its own meaning. Write what you think each part means. Nothing is marked; Check says how many ticked entries are right, not which.

          Transmit

          Build a sign from parts you have seen, then send it.
