Constellations

A dim observatory on Hollin Ridge, and a patch of real sky on the table. Some stars carry a number: that many lines must touch it. Join the stars without crossing any lines, and the figure that appears is a constellation. Nothing is timed and nothing can go wrong.

Charts

Each season is a page of the atlas. Five finished charts in a season open the next page; inside a season, pick any chart in any order. A chart shows its name only once you have finished it.

Sky Atlas

Every constellation you finish is drawn here at its real place in the sky (right ascension runs along the top, declination down the side). A dashed ring marks a chart still to find: its shape and name stay hidden until you finish it.

        Chart

        Keys: arrow keys move from star to star. Space or Enter picks up the star the ring is on; on a second star it draws the line (or erases it if it is there) and carries on from that star. Escape puts the star down. Ctrl or Cmd with Z undoes. With a mouse or finger, drag from one star to another, or tap one star and then the next; tap a line you drew to erase it.
