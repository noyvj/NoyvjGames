## One-line pitch
A night-sky logic puzzle: numbers on stars say how many lines must touch them, and the figure that falls out is a real constellation that you then keep in your Sky Atlas.

## Stack
- Pyodide Python, plain HTML/CSS, no build step. `game.py` is the single engine entry point (`handle(json) -> json`, `get_state()` / `load_state()` for the shared save widget); `app.js` is glue only (it draws SVG and turns input into requests). Python 3.9 locally (Pyodide 3.12): no 3.10+ syntax in engine code. No Node is available in this repo's tooling: check JS in the browser.
- Runs from any static server. No audio, no image files (every star, line and the atlas is drawn as SVG by the page).

## Core constraints (do not violate without asking)
1. The engine is pure and deterministic: no clock, no randomness (a test scans every engine module). Whether a figure is valid is a pure function of the chart and the lines drawn.
2. Nothing is timed, nothing can fail, nothing is lost. Lines are free to erase, undo and redo. A constellation in the atlas stays there. A wrong figure is described in words ("2 numbered stars still need more lines"), never called a failure.
3. No luck and no guessing: every chart is PROVED to have exactly one valid figure, equal to the traditional one, and to be finishable by line logic (at most one what-if step). A chart that fails is redrawn, never shipped.
4. Never colour alone: a numbered star is a badge with its number; a number that holds has a double ring and a tick, one with too many lines a dashed ring and "!"; a given line is a thick double rail; a crossed-out pair has an "x"; two crossing lines are dashed and marked with an "x"; a held star has a halo and its neighbours get dotted rings; the keyboard star has a heavy ring. Everything visual has a text version (cursor line, status line, "Chart as text").
5. Effects toggleable (star glow, the shine when a constellation is found); reduced motion honoured; light and dark theme; high contrast; text size.
6. Stars are real (HYG star database, drawn flat); figures are the traditional IAU/Stellarium ones. No generated or downloaded images.
7. A chart's name, its stars' names and its note stay out of the view until the chart is finished (a test checks); unfinished atlas places show only a dashed ring.

## The rules (`sky.py`)
- A chart is a set of stars at integer positions on a 1000-wide canvas, a list of CANDIDATE lines (the traditional figure plus each star's three nearest neighbours, minus any line that would run closer than 20 units to a third star), and clues: a number on a star (that many lines touch it; 0 means outside), MUST lines (already drawn, cannot be removed), NEVER lines (crossed-out pairs).
- Valid figure: every number holds exactly, no two lines cross, touch or run along each other (exact integer geometry, `edges_conflict`), no never-line is drawn, and all the lines form ONE connected figure. A star without a number may have any number of lines, including none. Given lines count as drawn.
- ONE exception, the Southern Cross (Crux): its traditional figure is two lines that cross, so its data carries `cross_ok: [[[0, 3], [1, 2]]]` (pairs of lines, as star pairs). A listed pair does not conflict, and where the two cross they count as JOINED (`_components`, `_groups`, `_reach` and the `close` rule know it; `count_by_stars` waives the same pair), so the cross is one piece. `tools/figures.crossing_pairs` finds such pairs in a figure and the builder writes the key only when there is one (every other chart is byte-identical). The page draws a small ring where the two meet (`b-joined`, from the view's `chart.joined`), and the chart text says the pair may cross. A test asserts only `crux` has the key.
- `analyse(chart, lines)` returns degree per star, over/under, crossing pairs, never-lines used, number of pieces, `valid`.

## How correctness is PROVED (`sky.py`, `tools/`, `tests/test_charts.py`)
- `solutions(chart, limit)`: the exhaustive solver (depth-first over the lines, pruned by the numbers and crossings, connectivity judged on each candidate). `count_by_stars(chart, limit)`: a second counter that shares no search code with it (star by star, line-against-line crossing tests, judged by `analyse`); the tests cross-check the two against brute force on small charts.
- Every chart: exactly one valid figure (both counters) and it equals the stored traditional figure; EVERY clue is needed (take any one number, given line or crossed-out pair away and a second figure appears); line logic finishes it (`logic` 1 = plain line logic, 2 = needs one what-if step). `propagate(chart, lookahead)` is the line logic a person can follow: `full` (a star has all its lines: the rest are out), `forced` (a star needs every line still open at it), `cross` (a line that would cross a drawn one is out), `close` (a line that would finish a piece while numbered stars remain outside it is out), `bridge` (the only way to join stars the figure must reach is in), and with lookahead one "what if I draw it" step. A test checks it never contradicts the figure. The hint ladder (`hints.py`) reads this.
- Geometry tests: whole-number positions inside the canvas, no two stars closer than 66 units, the figure connected, planar, every star on it; data tests: every star has a Hipparcos id, name and magnitude matching the snapshot; a few facts (brightest star of famous constellations, Orion's belt in a row, east on the left).

## Content (milestone 4: all five seasons, 40 charts)
Winter Sky (Auriga, Columba, Monoceros, Canis Major, Gemini, Taurus, Lepus, Orion), Spring Sky (Corvus, Cancer, Ursa Minor, Crater, Leo, Boötes, Virgo, Ursa Major), Summer Sky (Lyra, Delphinus, Sagitta, Corona Borealis, Ophiuchus, Aquila, Cygnus, Scorpius), Autumn Sky (Cassiopeia, Cepheus, Lacerta, Andromeda, Piscis Austrinus, Capricornus, Perseus, Pegasus), Southern Sky (Crux, Ara, Grus, Vela, Corona Australis, Pavo, Lupus, Centaurus, trimmed to 14 stars): 40 charts of 4 (Crux only) to 14 stars, each season ordered small to big. A `south: True` spec (the Southern Sky) says "best seen on evenings around X (from the southern hemisphere; low or out of sight from the north)"; Cassiopeia and Cepheus say they are visible all year from the northern middle latitudes. Every new chart passed the same proofs first time (one valid figure by both counters, every clue needed, line logic; 11 of the 16 need one what-if step). Each chart carries the traditional name, meaning, a short note written for the game, the brightest star, "best seen" month (the month it stands highest at 9 pm for northern middle latitudes, from its right ascension) and the atlas position.

## Gating
Season 0 is open from the start; season n+1 opens when `OPEN_AT` (5) charts of season n are done (any five); inside a season every chart is open in any order. Derived from the finished set, so it only ever goes up. A save is judged in season order on load, so a hand-edited save cannot skip ahead.

## For the local AI (exact names; every one was checked against the code)
- File map: `game.py` (entry: `handle(json) -> json`, `get_state()`, `load_state(d)`), engine modules `sky charts_s1..s5 charts hints goals achievements info` (the `ENGINE_MODULES` list at the top of `app.js`, in load order; `charts_s*.py` are GENERATED by `tools/build_charts.py`, never edit them), `app.js` (glue only), `index.html`, `style.css`, `settings.js`, `data/` (frozen star snapshot), `pc-config.json` + `pc.js` + `pc.css` (Desktop layout; `pc.html` is generated), `tools/` (dev only, never loaded).
- Actions of `handle()` (request `{"action": ..., ...}`): `open`; `pick {chart}` (a chart id such as "auriga"); `next`; `toggle {a, b}` (star indexes, integers from 0, A is 0; draws the line or erases it; one undo step); `undo`; `redo`; `clear`; `finish` (only when every number holds and the lines are one figure); `hint {confirm}` (confirm must be `true` on the third rung unless Easy); `hint_draw` (after rung 3); `difficulty {level}` (easy normal hard); `reset`. A refused request gives a normal view with `"ok": false` and a `message`; an unknown action gives `{"error": ...}`.
- View keys: `ok message say chart valid problems parts words editable can_undo can_redo can_clear just seasons atlas cards tally hint hint_ui goals difficulty achievements totals info`. `chart` keys: `id number of season season_index w h stars allowed never lines status joined moves best` (a star is `{i x y mag letter need have state}`; `allowed`, `never` and `lines` are lists of `[a, b]`; the name, meaning and note appear only once the chart is finished). `tally` `draws erases hints`; `totals` `charts done stars pages_open pages seasons_done lines erased hints`; `hint` is null or `{rung kind star nudge detail line answer ghost erase can_draw}`; `hint_ui` `available next label limit confirm opens_two can_draw here`; `atlas` `{w h items}`.
- `get_state()` keys (non-default only, plus `schema`, so a fresh game is `{"schema": 1}`): `schema cur solved work moves tally hints achievements_earned` (the last is written, never read back).
- Page: `render()` in `app.js` redraws everything after each `send(request)`; `window.constellationsRefresh` redraws after a save loads. Main ids: `#board` (SVG; each star is `[data-star="N"]`; keyboard: a to z jump to a star, Enter picks it up and joins it to the next star you Enter on, Escape puts it down), `#undo-button`, `#redo-button`, `#clear-button`, `#done-button` (Finish), `#done-card`, `#hint-button`, `#hint-draw-button`, `#prev-chart-button`, `#next-chart-button`, `#zoom-in-button`, `#zoom-out-button`, `#status-line`, `#cursor-line`, `#season-list`, `#atlas`, `#atlas-cards`, `#stats` (`#stat-done #stat-stars #stat-pages #stat-seasons #stat-lines #stat-hints`), `#goals`, `#toast`, `#announce`; `#engine-status` empties when the engine is ready.
