# Constellations: long history (NOTES.md)

This is the long history moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-18 (data pipeline, engine and page notes, hint ladder, difficulty and goals, standard kit, achievements, Desktop boot, hub registration). The local AI should read only the section it is told to; `CLAUDE.md` holds everything a change must not break.

## Data pipeline (dev tools, never run by the game)
- `tools/fetch_snapshot.py` reads the HYG star database v4.1 and the Stellarium "modern" sky culture (IAU figures as Hipparcos chains) and freezes the 380 stars and 40 figures used into `data/snapshot.json` (dated 2026-10-12; refreshed for milestone 4 with the same read date, and the 24 earlier figures and stars came back identical, so seasons 1 to 3 did not change).
- `tools/chart_specs.py` holds the hand-written side: which constellations go in which season, the card note, the stars to drop or the `cap` that trims long figures (Orion drops its faintest outlying shield and club stars; Ursa Major, Gemini and Canis Major are trimmed to 14, 12 and 12 stars).
- `tools/figures.py`: gnomonic projection about the figure's centre (east on the left), scaled onto the canvas, then `spread` pushes stars apart so none is closer than 68 units or sits within 34 units of a figure line it does not belong to (the chart is a drawing, not a survey), candidate lines, and `derive_clues` / `best_clues`: start with a number on every star, add must/never lines only if needed, then take clues away one at a time while the figure stays the only one; several removal orders are tried and the set that line logic finishes with the least reasoning and the most numbers wins.
- `tools/build_charts.py` writes `charts_s1.py` to `charts_s5.py` (GENERATED, do not edit) and refuses to write a chart that is not uniquely, logically solvable. A test rebuilds three charts and compares.
Adding a season: add it to `chart_specs.SEASONS`, run `tools/fetch_snapshot.py` (needs the two source files or the network; pass `--hyg` and `--stellarium` paths to keep the dated read), then `tools/build_charts.py`, add the new `charts_sN.py` to `charts._MODULES` and to `ENGINE_MODULES` in `app.js` (a test checks the list), then the tests.

## Engine and page notes
- `game.handle` returns the whole view. Actions: `open`, `pick {chart}`, `next` (next open unfinished chart), `toggle {a, b}` (draw the line between stars a and b, or erase it; one call = one undo step; refuses a pair that is not allowed, a given line or a crossed-out pair, with a message and no count), `undo`, `redo`, `clear` (erase all my lines on the chart, one undo step), `finish` (only when valid), `hint {confirm}`, `hint_draw`, `difficulty {level}`, `reset`. Stars are lettered A, B, C ... from left to right.
- Save (`get_state`, only non-default keys besides `schema`): `cur`, `solved {id: line changes}`, `work {id: ["3-7", ...]}` (unfinished chart's drawn lines; given lines are never written), `moves`, `tally {draws, erases, hints}`, `hints {id: highest rung}`. Undo history is not saved. `load` checks every field alone, drops charts in closed seasons, keeps only real allowed lines that are not given or crossed-out, and finishes a saved figure that is already complete. A `schema` key (1) is always written; a bad or newer schema gives a fresh game.
- Stats strip: Constellations (x/40), Stars joined (stars in finished figures), Atlas pages (seasons open), Seasons done, Lines drawn (the tally counts lines drawn and erased), Hints asked.
- Page: SVG board drawn by `app.js` (`drawBoard`): stars sized by magnitude, a badge with the number on numbered stars, lines, given lines, never pairs, ghost lines to a held star's neighbours, crossing marks. Input: drag from a star to another (a rubber band follows), or tap one star then another (the second stays held, so a tap chain draws a path), tap a line you drew to erase it, keyboard (arrows move to the nearest star in that direction, Enter/Space picks up a star and on a second star draws or erases and carries on from it, Esc puts it down, a letter key jumps to that star, Home/End, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, with Shift redo). A press counts as on a star within 54 units. The board is a square-ish SVG that scales with the width (down to 360 px; the 14-star charts are the tightest at about 24 px between stars on a phone: see Board size below).
- Charts panel (`#charts-panel`): each season with found/total, locked seasons say how many more to finish, a found chart shows its name, an unfound one only "Chart n" and its star count. Sky Atlas (`#atlas-panel`): equirectangular, 4 units per degree, right ascension runs right to left, a finished figure is drawn at its real place with its name, an unfound chart is a small dashed ring labelled "Winter 3"; click or Enter opens the card (finished) or goes to the chart (open season). Cards (`<details>`): season, stars, brightest star, best month, line changes, the note, sources with the read date.
- Settings: star letters (on), star names on finished charts (off), text size, reduce motion, effects, high contrast, theme, reset display, start the atlas over (confirm dialog). About: pledge, how it works, three reworded facts with the Wikipedia source and read date 2026-10-12, plus a live read of the Wikipedia summary of "IAU designated constellations" when About is opened (with a stated fallback). What's New (`changelog.json`), `sources.json` for the hub's sources page.
- Engine modules (loaded in this order by `app.js`): sky, charts_s1..s5, charts, hints, goals, achievements, info, then game. A test checks the list.

## Hint ladder (`hints.py`, milestone 4)
- Opens only on a press of the suggestion button ("Would you like a suggestion?", the owner's wording); `hint {confirm}` in `game.py`; the view carries `hint` (only the rungs pressed so far) and `hint_ui` (what the button offers next, its label, whether a confirm is needed, the Hard limit, `can_draw`). Alt+H asks too (a plain H is a star letter on charts of 8 or more stars).
- 1 nudge: "Star C has a line that is certain" (a star, no line; the board rings it with a dashed ring). 2 hint: ONE line, drawn as a dotted ghost with a "+" plate (a "-" plate for a line to erase), and the reasoning in words (`forced`: the star needs N lines and only N joins are left, with the excluded joins and why from the trail; `bridge`; `what-if`, the one what-if step a level-2 chart needs). 3 answer: every missing line as ghosts, a "Lines to draw: ..." text, and a "Draw it" button (`hint_draw`): erases any lines that do not belong and draws the rest as ONE undo step, not counted in the tally of lines drawn or erased; Done is still the player's to press. Normal asks a confirm before showing the answer.
- The line comes from `sky.propagate` run on the player's CORRECT lines only (a line that is not in the figure is ignored; it is named only, as a "remove" hint, when nothing is left to draw), plain logic first, then one what-if step only if the chart's stored `logic` is 2: so a hint is always justified and deterministic. A test presses through the ladder alone on EVERY chart and checks every hinted line is in the figure and the chart always finishes; another checks level-1 charts never open with a what-if.
- Counting: `tally.hints` counts every press that opened something; `hints {chart: highest rung}` records the highest rung asked while a chart was first being solved, saved as `hints` (milestone 6's "No Hints Needed" reads it). Any change to the lines, the chart or the level clears the ladder.

## Difficulty, goals, zoom, live reads (milestone 4)
- Difficulty: `shared/difficulty.js` (`NoyvjDifficulty.mount("#difficulty-slot", {game: "constellations"})`, per-browser `difficulty:constellations`), told to the engine with `{action: "difficulty", level}`, a module global that `reset` and `load` leave alone and that is never saved. Easy: the first press opens nudge and hint together, no confirm for the answer. Hard: no answer step. It changes only how the ladder opens (a test scripts the same play on all three and requires identical totals, goals and saves).
- Goals (`goals.py`, the shared `goals-panel.js` shows three at a time): "Find 5 constellations in <season>" (opens the next page), lines drawn (50, 250, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000), "Find all 8 in <season>", total found (10, 25, 40); not-done goals first in that rotation, all numbers only go up. A Settings checkbox hides the strip.
- Board size (phone density): `+` / `-` / `Fit` under the chart scale the board to 150, 200 or 300 percent of the width; it scrolls sideways inside the board frame (`.board-wrap.zoomed`), the cursor is scrolled into view by keyboard play, the choice is kept per device (`constellations:zoom`). While zoomed, the SVG gets `touch-action: pan-x pan-y`, so on a touch screen a drag scrolls and a tap on one star then another draws (mouse drag-to-draw is unchanged). 14-star charts at 360 px go from about 24 px between stars to 48 px or 72 px.
- Live reads (About panel, when opened, each with its source and the day it was read, and a quiet stated fallback): Wikipedia's summary of "IAU designated constellations" (milestone 3) and, new, today's Stellarium "modern" sky culture file (`raw.githubusercontent.com/Stellarium/stellarium/.../modern/index.json`, about 200 KB): for every finished constellation it checks that its stars are still on the live figure (engine cards carry `code` and `hips`) and says how many still match. Star positions and brightness stay the dated snapshot (the star database is 30 MB, too large to read each time); the About text says so.

## Achievements (milestone 6: `achievements.py`, `achievements.json`)
- 14, the hub-wide framework: `achievements.json` is the manifest (kept equal to the `ACHIEVEMENTS` table in `achievements.py` by a test), `achievements_earned` is a write-only projection in `get_state()` (only written when something is earned; `load` never reads it back), and the view carries `achievements` (id, label, description, earned, have, need). Every achievement is computed from state the save already keeps (the finished set, the `hints` record, the tally of lines drawn): `facts_of(solved, hinted, draws)` then `earned(facts)` / `view(facts)`. The module never reads difficulty or the engine; a test requires the same earned set and numbers on Easy, Normal, Hard and the default.
- The list: First Figure (1), Ten Figures, Twenty Figures, Forty Figures (all 40), Winter / Spring / Summer / Autumn / Southern Done (all 8 of that season; read from the real season sizes), "Belt and Sword" (id `the_hunter`: Orion), The Bears (Ursa Major and Ursa Minor, both), "Smallest of All" (id `southern_cross`: Crux), No Hints Needed (10 constellations finished with no suggestion asked while they were first being solved), Long Night (500 lines drawn; draws and erases of the player's own count, the ladder's "Draw it" does not).
- **Names stay hidden.** The plan called three of them "The Hunter", "The Bears" and "Southern Cross". The achievement list is part of the view and core constraint 7 (an unfinished chart's name, meaning, note and star names stay out of it; `test_game.py` scans the whole view) forbids naming a chart before it is found, and "The Hunter" is exactly Orion's meaning. So the three are worded by what the player will see: "Belt and Sword" (a belt of three bright stars in a row, winter), "The Bears" (kept: both bears of the spring sky, one large and one small) and "Smallest of All" (a cross of four stars low in the southern sky; Crux is the smallest of the 88). A test checks that no achievement text names or gives the meaning of any constellation or star. The ids keep the plan's names. To use the plan's labels the owner must accept that the list names unfound charts (one table row and one manifest entry each).
- Long Night is reachable by plain play: finishing all 40 charts draws 322 lines (352 figure lines minus 30 given), and the rest comes from mistakes drawn and taken back on any open chart. The reachability test scripts exactly that on Normal (and on Easy/Hard/default).
- UI: a toolbar button ("Achievements (n/14)", "Trophies n/14" when narrow) and `#achievements-panel` with the text count "N of 14 achievements earned", one row per achievement with "Earned" (double border, never colour alone) or "have of need"; the shared `achievement-share.js` adds a Share button on earned rows. A toast and an announcer line say "Achievement unlocked: <label>." once when one is newly earned (`knownEarned` is null until the first view, and a loaded save resets it, so loading never toasts); `send()` appends it after the move's own message (so "Auriga is in your atlas." does not hide it). Escape closes the panel.

## Desktop boot (milestone 7: `pc-config.json`, `pc.css`, `pc.js`, generated `pc.html`)
- `pc.html` is generated (`scripts/generate-pc-pages.py`, never edited by hand); `index.html` gained the `layout-pref.js` line and the `window.constellationsTutorialSteps` hook (app.js asks it which tutorial to run; pc.js defines `CONSTELLATIONS_PC_TUTORIAL_STEPS`). Same `game.py`, same engine modules, same game id, so saves are shared.
- Layout (1440x900, and 1024x700 where the stats take their own row): top bar with the back link, the title, the six stats and the toolbar icons (Charts, Sky Atlas, Achievements, Settings) plus the Menu (Tutorial, About, Credits, Report a problem, What's New, Fullscreen, Classic layout). Stage: the chart's title line, the sky board filling every remaining pixel (the SVG is absolutely positioned inside `#board-wrap`, `preserveAspectRatio` letterboxes, pointer input uses `getScreenCTM`, so drawing works at any size), then the status line and the cursor line. Right column: the three goals, Prev/Next, Undo/Redo/Erase/Done, the done card, the suggestion ladder, "Chart as text", the difficulty choice. Windows: Charts, Sky Atlas (with its cards), Achievements, What's New, Settings, About. Hidden but still in the page (their ids are used by `app.js`): the tagline, the keys note and the "Board size" row (the larger-board buttons are for small screens; `applyZoom` forces 100 percent when `data-layout="pc"`, and the saved Classic choice is left alone).
- Messages (`#toast`) float at the bottom centre instead of taking a row. The Desktop tutorial has nine steps, every selector an id on pc.html (a test checks).
- Known limits: the shell opens its Menu on Escape when no window is open, in the capture phase, so Escape while holding a star both puts the star down and opens the Menu (close it with Escape again); the board letterboxes tall 14-star charts at 1024x700 (about 520 px wide); the side column scrolls when the difficulty panel is open.
- `tests/test_desktop_boot.py` (14 tests) checks pc.html equals the generator output, both pages share the id set `app.js` needs, every toolbar button is an icon or Menu entry, every panel is a window, the tutorial selectors exist, the zones, the keys listed are real, the board-fill rules and that pc.css is scoped.
