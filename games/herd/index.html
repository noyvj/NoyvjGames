Grow a farm. Herd size and methane emissions are coupled by default — decoupling them takes deliberate investment.

Methane is a far more potent greenhouse gas than CO2 over the short term — roughly 80x stronger over 20 years — which makes industrial agriculture's methane output an outsized lever for near-term warming, even though it fades from the atmosphere faster than CO2 does.
