    How much event damage you avoid. Each Resilience investment adds a flat 5%, capped at 85% total even if you max it out — Early Warning Systems adds another flat 10% on top once unlocked. It never reaches 100%; some damage always gets through.

    Growth doesn't protect you from event damage at all — each unit just adds 8 resources of passive income every time you face an event. It's a bet that the extra income outweighs skipping a point of damage mitigation.

    Knowledge earned per run is your leftover resources divided by 20, rounded — but never zero, even after a terrible run. Unlocked skills below are permanent: they carry into every future run, unlike Resilience/Growth investment, which resets to a fresh start each run. Some skills require others to be unlocked first.

    Adaptive Growth Practices — +1 starting growth capacity
