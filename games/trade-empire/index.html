Market prices

i

Selling a good repeatedly crashes its price — each unit sold nudges that good's price down, and it only recovers gradually on its own if you leave it alone for a while. Spreading sales across goods keeps prices higher than dumping everything on one.

Colonies

i

A colony's output scales with how well its need is being met, from 0.5x up to 1.5x — an under-supplied colony produces less to trade. Keep delivering enough of what it needs and it develops to Level 2, gaining a second need that reaches into the other supply cycle.

Fleet priority

i

Only affects automated ships that are idle and empty. When on, they'll abandon their fixed shuttle route and reposition themselves toward whichever producer feeds the fleet's most under-served colony, instead of always reloading the same local good.
