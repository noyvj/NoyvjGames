Run a coastal settlement. Balance fishing income against ocean health and a slowly rising sea.

Ocean acidification and sea-level rise share a real-world trait this game leans on: the delay between cause and effect. Today's emissions and choices don't show their full damage for years — which is exactly why they're easy to underestimate.

    Coastal programmes

    Coastal heritage

    Two sites sit on the coast. Protecting one costs a one-off sum plus upkeep each season, but it survives even if its row floods. Unprotected sites are lost when their row goes under.

    Citizen-science monitoring

    Fund volunteer monitoring crews (one report every 2 seasons at most). Each report reveals a real-world finding about ocean acidification and sea level.

    Managed retreat

    A different strategy from building higher: deliberately clear the lowest dry row (it shows hatched, as flooded) and rehouse people inland. Each of up to 2 steps permanently cuts sea-level damage by a further 20%, but you keep less coast, and heritage on that row is lost.

    Economy diversification

    Tourism and aquaculture add income that does not ride the delayed fish-stock crash. Tourism shrinks as coast is lost and gains from protected heritage; aquaculture loses a little to acidity straight away.

    Checkpoint & replay

    Save a checkpoint now. Later, replay from it: the run rewinds to the checkpoint, and you are shown what the abandoned run actually brought in the seasons ahead (fish yield, damage, flooding) so you can choose differently.
