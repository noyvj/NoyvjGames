Reaction Bench

A night-shift lab on a quiet research station. An order board asks for compounds; you turn the dials until the atoms on both sides are equal. Nothing is timed and nothing can go wrong.

The order board

Each order is one equation. A chapter opens when five orders of the one before are on the shelf; inside a chapter, take them in any order.

The shelf

The periodic table

A tile lights the first time an order that uses that element is run. Open a lit tile to read its card. Faint tiles are not on this bench and are not needed.

            Keys: Tab moves between the dials; Up and Right turn a dial up, Down and Left turn it down, Home and End go to 1 and 12, and the digits 1 to 9 set it.
