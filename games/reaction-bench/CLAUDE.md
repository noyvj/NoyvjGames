# Reaction Bench (working title)

Seed: `planning/reaction-bench-plan.md` (Quick ideas round A4, TODO QI-4). Personal project, no BCM tag. Milestones 1 to 5 built 2026-10-12 by background agents (about 390 tests in `tests/`: `python3 -m pytest -q tests` from this folder): engine and proofs, the playable bench, chapters 2 and 3 with the periodic table, then chapters 4 and 5 with the two-step routes, the hint ladder, the goals strip, difficulty and live PubChem facts (milestone 4, the first complete game), and the standard kit (milestone 5). Milestones 6 and 7 (achievements, Desktop boot) are not built.

## One-line pitch
A quiet night-shift lab on a research station: balance each chemical equation with dials, put the compound on the shelf, and light a new tile on the periodic table for every element you use.

## Stack
- Pyodide Python, plain HTML/CSS, no build step. `game.py` is the single engine entry point (`handle(json) -> json`, `get_state()` / `load_state()` for the shared save widget); `app.js` is glue only (it draws flasks, dials, the ledger, the shelf and the table, and turns input into requests). Python 3.9 locally (Pyodide 3.12): no 3.10+ syntax in engine code. No Node is available in this repo's tooling: check JS in the browser.
- Runs from any static server. No audio, no image files: every flask, atom chip and table tile is drawn as SVG by code.

## Core constraints (do not violate without asking)
1. The engine is pure and deterministic: no clock, no randomness, no shuffling (a test scans every engine module). The ledger, the balance check and the gate are pure functions of the equation and the dials; candidate product lists are in a fixed order.
2. Nothing is timed, nothing can fail, nothing is lost. A wrong setting only shows what is off; a shelved compound stays shelved; Run can be repeated; Reset dials is free.
3. No luck and no guessing: every reaction is PROVED (below). A reaction that fails is rewritten, never shipped.
4. Never colour alone: reactants go in conical flasks and products come out of round ones, each species is labelled "Goes in" or "Comes out"; a ledger row is a tick and the word "Equal", or a cross and "n too many / too few on the right" with a signed number; a tried product list is struck through, dashed and carries its reason in words; an atom chip is a shape (circle, square, triangle, diamond, pentagon, hexagon by atomic number) plus the symbol; a lit tile shows its symbol, a dark one only its number and "?", a faint one "not on this bench". Everything visual has a text version (the equation in words, the ledger as a real table with row labels, "The table as text").
5. Effects toggleable (the glow when a compound reaches the shelf); reduced motion honoured; light and dark theme; high contrast; text size.
6. Safety: only everyday, textbook-safe chemistry, equations only. `chem.lint` (banned words and banned formulas such as KClO3, NH4NO3, HCN, CO, NO2) runs over every reaction's words and formulas in the tests, and a test rejects quantities, apparatus and "how to" wording.

## How correctness is PROVED (`chem.py`, `tools/balance.py`, `tests/test_reactions.py`)
- `parse_tree` / `count_atoms`: the formula parser (element symbols checked against the real 118, nested brackets, repeated elements added up, errors raised for anything unreadable). `ledger(reactants, products, coefs)` is what the player sees (difference = right minus left).
- `nullspace`: exact Fraction Gaussian elimination. `solutions(r, p, limit=12)` lists every setting from 1 to 12 that balances; `smallest` is the one in lowest terms. For every authored reaction a test asserts: nullspace dimension exactly 1, `smallest` equals the stored answer, the stored answer passes the player's ledger and is in lowest terms and inside the 1 to 12 dial range, and every other balancing setting is a multiple of it.
- A second, independent route: `tools/balance.py brute_force` tries every setting (for up to four species) and must find only the stored answer. A third: mass. `elements.MASSES` (bundled PubChem snapshot, decimal strings) gives exactly equal left and right mass as Fractions (and within 0.01).
- Product-choice rounds: each has four candidate lists in a fixed order with the right one at a fixed index (all four positions are used); a test proves exactly one list can be balanced (`chem.can_balance`), and every wrong list gets a computed reason: an element on the right that is not on the left, an element on the left with nowhere to go, or "every element is on both sides, but no whole-number settings make them all come out equal" (all three kinds occur).
- `python3 tools/balance.py [chapter-id]` prints the proof table.

## Content
`reactions_c1.py` Bench Basics (8, whole equation shown: CO2, table salt, water, magnesium oxide, rust, aluminium oxide, magnesium nitride, aluminium chloride), `reactions_c2.py` Burning (8 product rounds: methane, propane, ethanol, pentane, ethyne, sulfur, glucose, ammonia in oxygen), `reactions_c3.py` Oxides and Salts (8 product rounds with bracket groups: limestone to lime, slaked lime, limewater, baking soda, peroxide, aluminium hydroxide, wet rust step, ammonium sulfate), `reactions_c4.py` Acids, Bases and Swaps (8 product rounds: neutralisation, milk of magnesia, potassium sulfate, fizz, a metal in acid, chalk from two salts, copper beats silver, calcium phosphate), `reactions_c5.py` Works (8 product rounds forming four two-step routes: sulfuric acid, fertiliser, iron, sugar to fuel): 40 frozen reactions (id, title, reactants, products, answer, choices/right, a plain fact shown once shelved, one dry line from Fennel). `reactions.py` wraps them (`CHAPTERS`, `ALL`, `BY_ID`, `ORDER`, `NAMES` for every formula, `BENCH_ELEMENTS`) and holds the gating. 15 bench elements (H C N O Na Mg Al P S Cl K Ca Fe Cu Ag; the plan's 36 was not reachable with 40 safe orders, so milestone 6's table achievements should scale to 15). The dial range stays 1 to 12 (decided in milestone 4: widening it would need dial keys beyond the digits and a wider 360 px row, and 40 reactions fit inside 12, the largest coefficient being 8): a reaction whose lowest answer needs 13 or more (butane, octane) cannot be authored; pentane stands in. The brute-force proof now covers orders of up to five species (`tests/test_reactions.py`).
Adding a chapter: write `reactions_cN.py`, add it to `reactions._MODULES` and to `ENGINE_MODULES` in `app.js` (a test checks the list), add any new formula to `NAMES` and any new element to `elements.BENCH`, run `tools/balance.py`, then the tests. Decoy product lists must not hold a banned formula (CO, NO2 ...: `chem.lint` runs over every list) and must not balance by accident (a script such as `chem.can_balance` over the draft lists finds out; e.g. SO2 + H2O2 balances against SO3 + H2O).

## Two-step routes (chapter 5)
Each route is two orders next to each other (data: `route`, `step` 1 or 2, `carries` on step 1; `ROUTES` in `reactions_c5.py` names them). `Reaction.after` is the step-one id; `reactions.reaction_open` also needs it on the shelf and `route_locked` says a step two is waiting. What step one makes (`carries`, one of its products) is a reactant of step two: the view's `reaction.route` has the note, the species are flagged `carried` ("Goes in (carried over)" / "Comes out (carried on)"), the order board shows "Needs step 1" (aria-disabled, with the word), `next` and the page's prev/next skip locked step twos, and `load` judges the shelf in order so a saved step two without step one is dropped. Chapter gating (any five of the chapter before) is unchanged.

## Gating
Chapter 0 is open from the start; chapter n+1 opens when `OPEN_AT` (5) orders of chapter n are on the shelf (any five). Inside a chapter every order is open in any order. Derived from the shelf, so it only ever goes up. A save is judged in chapter order on load, so a hand-edited save cannot skip ahead.

## Engine and page notes
- `game.handle` returns the whole view. Actions: `open`, `pick {reaction}`, `next` (next open order not on the shelf), `dial {index, value | delta}` (1 to 12; refused before the product is chosen), `reset_dials`, `choose {option}` (a product round: a wrong list never costs anything), `run` (only when the products are known and the ledger reads equal; adds one run, puts the order on the shelf, lights the tiles of its elements), `reset`.
- Every dial turn that changes a number, every Run and every wrong list tried counts in the tally (`turns`, `runs`, `tries`); the stat strip shows Compounds shelved, Elements found, Dial turns and Runs. A balanced setting that is not in lowest terms still runs, and the ledger says it could be divided down.
- The view never contains an unshelved order's answer or fact (a test checks), a closed chapter's titles or lines, or a dark tile's symbol or name. Elements found are DERIVED from the shelf (nothing separate to save). Tiles: `found` (symbol, name, card), `bench` (dark: number only), `off` (faint: symbol and name, "not on this bench"). The table has all 118 places in the usual 18-column layout with the lanthanides and actinides beneath and marker cells; the element card shows name, number, group, period, kind, state, atomic mass (bundled PubChem snapshot read 2026-10-12, labelled as such), a one-line use, the shelved orders that use it and how many open ones still do.
- Save (`get_state`, only non-default keys besides `schema`): `cur`, `shelf {id: runs}`, `dials {id: [..]}` (only when not all 1), `picked [ids]` (rounds chosen but not yet shelved), `tried {id: [indices]}`, `first [ids]` (rounds chosen right first time: for milestone 6's "Right Product"), `tally {turns, runs, tries}`. `load` checks every field alone, drops orders in closed chapters, ignores a bad `dials` list, and re-derives `picked` for shelved rounds. A `schema` key (1) is always written; a missing schema is read as layout 1, a bad or newer schema is ignored (a fresh game).
- Page: the equation row is built once per order (and rebuilt when the product is chosen) and then updated in place so keyboard focus stays on the dial. Each dial is a labelled `spinbutton` (ArrowUp/Right and ArrowDown/Left, PageUp/PageDown by 3, Home 1, End 12, digits 1 to 9) with minus and plus buttons beside it for touch (those two are out of the tab order). Keys elsewhere: R runs, N next order, [ and ] step back and forward, 1 to 4 choose a product list. The ledger is a real table; its chip trays are drawn per element (capped at 30, then "+n"). Panels: Orders (the board), Shelf (flasks), Periodic table (+ card + "The table as text"), What's New, Settings (Fennel's remarks on or off, text size, reduced motion, effects, high contrast, theme, reset), About (`info.py`: pledge, how it works, four reworded facts with sources and read dates, plus a live read of the Wikipedia summary of "Chemical equation").
- Engine modules (loaded in this order by `app.js`): elements, chem, reactions_c1..c5, reactions, info, hints, goals, then game. A test checks the list and the import order.

## Hint ladder, goals, difficulty (milestone 4)
- `hints.py`: three rungs, each opening only on a press of "Would you like a suggestion?" (`hint {confirm}` in `game.py`; the view has `hint` (rungs pressed so far) and `hint_ui` (what the button offers next)). In a product round: 1 nudge (what goes in), 2 hint (the first wrong list not yet tried, with its reason), 3 answer (the right list is chosen for you; not counted as "right first time"). Once the dials are open: 1 nudge (the unequal element in the fewest formulas, and how it reads), 2 hint (one dial and what it should read; that dial gets a dashed border), 3 answer (every dial set to the stored answer; Run is still the player's). Normal asks first for rung 3, Easy opens rungs 1 and 2 on the first press and skips the ask, Hard stops after rung 2 (`difficulty {level}`, a module global that `reset` and `load` leave alone, never saved; changing it clears a ladder in progress). Any dial change, wrong try, choice, run or change of order clears the ladder. A test follows the hints dial by dial on every one of the 40 orders and requires it to finish.
- Counting: `tally.hints` counts every press that opened something; `hints {order id: highest rung}` (only while not shelved) is saved for milestone 6's "Straight Through". A shelved or already-equal order offers no suggestion. A Settings checkbox hides the suggestion box; the H key asks.
- `goals.py`: the queue the shared `goals-panel.js` shows three at a time (open the next chapter, light 5/10/all tiles, finish the chapter in hand, shelve 10/20/30/all compounds, turn the dials 100/500/2,000 times), done ones last. A Settings checkbox hides it. Stats strip: Compounds shelved, Elements found, Dial turns, Runs, Hints asked.
- Live facts: the element card reads the PubChem Periodic Table JSON (`PUBCHEM_URL` in `app.js`, CORS open) once, the first time a card or the table is opened, and shows atomic mass, kind, state and year of discovery with "read live ... on <date>"; if the read fails it says one quiet line and the bundled snapshot (the proofs use only the snapshot) stands.
- Wrapped rows: each sign sits in a `.term` with the formula after it, so a narrow row can start with + or an arrow but never end with one.
