              How the stall works
              • Customers ask for goods. Tap a crate to put a basic good on the counter (crates are free and never run out), then merge two identical goods into the next tier.
              • Tap a good, then tap a customer who wants it (or drag it over). A good of the right family and the tier asked for, or better, is accepted; a Critic wants exactly that tier.
              • Customers wait in beats, not seconds: a beat is a crate, a merge, a sweep or a hand-over. Moving, swapping, selling and thinking are free, so nothing happens while you pause.
              • Three identical goods together jump two tiers. A merge that lines up with a neighbour keeps going, as a chain, and each extra link pays a few coins.
              • Serve customers in a row without anyone leaving and the combo climbs to x2 and x3, which multiplies what they pay. Every fourth order in a row sets a wildcard on the counter: it merges with any good below a showpiece. A customer leaving only resets the combo for the rest of the day; nothing is lost.
              • Sell turns any good into coins and Broom sweeps it away, so the counter can never jam.
              • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter picks up or drops, D hands the picked-up good over, B is the broom, S sells, 1-3 open a crate, Esc lets go.
