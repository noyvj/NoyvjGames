Your counter

Open a crate to put a good on the counter. Two identical goods merge into a better one.

How the counter works
  • Tap a crate to put a basic good on the counter. Crates are free and never run out.
  • Tap a good, then tap an identical one (or drag it on) to merge them into the next tier. A tier-5 showpiece does not merge.
  • Three identical goods together jump two tiers. A merge that lines up with a neighbour keeps going, as a chain.
  • Tap a good then an empty cell to move it; drag one onto a different good to swap them.
  • Sell turns any good into coins and Broom sweeps it away for free, so the counter can never jam.
  • Keyboard: arrows move, Enter picks up or drops, B is the broom, S sells, 1-3 open a crate, Esc lets go.
