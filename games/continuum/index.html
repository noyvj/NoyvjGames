i

The land can only give so much per season. Harvesting more than that sustainable amount degrades land health, which cuts every future harvest — staying under the line lets it recover instead. This is what lets a small, careful settlement out-score a big, careless one.

Sustainability: 0 / 100

i

Every input to this score is a ratio — food per person, shelter per person — never a raw count, so growing the settlement without growing what it needs to support that size won't raise it. It's the average of the four equally-weighted components below.

i

Equity isn't an average of your three basic provisions (food, shelter, gathering space) — it's capped by whichever one is met worst, and docked further when provision is lopsided. Grand shelters don't help this number if people are still going hungry.

Research

Knowledge: 0.0

One tree, fourteen tiers, seven eras. What you lean into now changes what opens later.

i

A tier only opens once two nodes in the tier below it are researched — you don't need everything in a tier, just enough to prove the ground beneath it. Some nodes also require a minimum number of discoveries already made in one branch, so leaning into a branch early can open or close doors many tiers later.
