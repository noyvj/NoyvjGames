Hull Repair

Join matching ports with lines that never cross. Nothing is timed, and a wrong line only costs you a tap to take it back.

Station

Every room you patch lights up on the map. A deck opens once five rooms of the one before are patched. Inside a deck, play any room in any order. Patched means every line is joined; restored means every cell is covered too. Tap a room on the map, or pick it from the lists below.

          How drawing works
          • Drag from a port to lay a line, and drop it on the matching port. Lines move one cell at a time and cannot cross or share a cell.
          • Drag back along your own line to shorten it. Drag across another line to cut it. Tap the end of a line to take one cell back.
          • Keyboard: arrow keys move the cursor, Enter or Space picks a line up and puts it down, Backspace takes a cell back, Z undoes, Escape lets go.
