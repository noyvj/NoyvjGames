A forest region. Clear it for quick income, or leave it standing and let its value compound.

Land-use change and the loss of carbon sinks is one of the largest drivers of emissions worldwide — and "assisted natural regeneration" (letting cleared land recover with light human help, rather than replanting from scratch) is a real, cost-effective restoration approach this mechanic loosely echoes.

    i

    Every time a plot gets cleared, its future value growth is permanently reduced a little — clearing the same plot over and over makes it worse, though it never stops producing entirely.

    i

    A replanted plot takes a fixed number of ticks to finish recovering, then grows in value just like a never-cleared plot — restoration works, it's just slower.

    Preserved and Recovered plots quietly build up biodiversity the longer they stand undisturbed; once it crosses a threshold, a small wildlife icon appears on the tile — a sign the plot has been thriving a while.
