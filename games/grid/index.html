    Power Plants

    i

    Solar, Wind, and Hydro get cheaper each time you build another one of that type — a real-world "learning curve" effect — floored at 40% of their starting price. Coal, Gas, and Nuclear costs never change.

    Maintenance & Aging

    i

    Every standing plant quietly gets older each round. Past 8 rounds old, your oldest fleet risks a costly breakdown that takes a unit offline — Maintain spends funds to lower that plant type's average age instead of resetting it to zero, buying time rather than making it new again.

    Retiring refunds 50% of a plant's current cost, not what you originally paid for it — renewables get cheaper the more you build, so retiring one later refunds less than an early one cost to build.

    Maintain lowers a plant type's average age by a fixed amount, it doesn't reset it to zero — an older fleet needs repeat maintenance to stay ahead of rising breakdown risk.

    i

    Storage arbitrage: in Charge mode, batteries bank surplus generation (power above demand that would otherwise earn nothing), losing 15% in the round trip. In Discharge mode they sell stored power into a shortfall at a 1.5x peak price. Storage holds twice the battery fleet's capacity and moves at most the fleet's capacity per round. It never changes emissions or disruption risk.

    Battery storage doesn't generate power — it buffers renewable output when the weather isn't cooperating. It only matters with Weather Variability turned on above; without real intermittency to buffer, there's nothing for it to do.
