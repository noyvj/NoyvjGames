Slingshot

Kestrel Desk plans the burns; a probe called Wren flies them. Choose when, which way and how hard, watch the whole flight appear on the chart, and reach the Moon, Mars and its moons on a fuel budget. Nothing is timed and a miss costs nothing.

Missions

Five finished missions in a chapter open the next chapter; inside a chapter, pick any mission in any order.

Moon Atlas

Every place you can go, nearest the Sun on the left. Not to scale. A mark is one mission: a filled mark with a tick is done, with the fuel you used; an empty one is still to do. Press a mark to go to its mission.

          What this plan does

              Opening Kestrel Desk...

              Keys: with the chart focused, Left and Right move the time by one step, Page Up and Page Down by ten, Home and End jump to the start and end. B adds a burn at that time. F flies the plan. Ctrl or Cmd with Z undoes, with Shift redoes. Escape closes an open panel.
