Overlay window mockups

A test page for the Desktop version: the same sample window shown three ways. Open each, try it with the keyboard (Tab stays inside, Esc closes) and at a narrow width, then press "I prefer this one" on the one you like. It only stores your choice in this browser and shows it below so you can tell me. Nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Side drawer

  • Slides in from the right edge
  • The game stays visible and a little dimmed on the left
  • Good for settings and lists you glance at

2. Centred pop-up

  • A window in the middle with the game dimmed behind
  • Feels like a dialog; keeps focus on one thing
  • Good for short choices and questions

3. Full-screen menu

  • Covers the whole window, content in a calm column
  • Most room, best on a phone-sized window
  • Good for long menus and settings

Today's Desktop windows are closest to the centred pop-up. The style you pick would be used for every Menu entry, so the controls inside would stay the same.
